# Privacy Policy

RecomBox is a desktop and mobile application.

- **Data Collection**: RecomBox only requests access to Google account data necessary for its functionality (e.g., files you open or create). No unrelated personal data is collected.
- **Data Usage**: Access tokens are used solely to interact with Google APIs on your behalf. We do not sell, share, or use your data for advertising.
- **Data Storage**: Tokens are stored securely on your device or backend server for session management. We do not permanently store your files.
- **User Control**: You may revoke access at any time via your Google Account settings.
- **Contact**: For questions, email [dev.khbyte@gmail.com](mailto:dev.khbyte@gmail.com).
