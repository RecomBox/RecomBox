# Privacy Policy

RecomBox is a desktop and mobile application.

- **Data Collection**: RecomBox collects your email address and username when you sign in. It may also request access to Google account data necessary for its functionality (e.g., Google drive internal app files/folders).
- **Data Usage**: Collected information is used solely to provide app functionality, personalize your experience, and enable secure access to Google APIs. We do not sell, share, or use your data for advertising.
- **Data Storage**: Email and username are stored securely on your device or backend server. Access tokens are stored only for session management. We do not permanently store your files.
- **User Control**: You may revoke access at any time via your Google Account settings. You may also request deletion of your account data by contacting us.
- **Contact**: For questions or requests, email [dev.khbyte@gmail.com](mailto:dev.khbyte@gmail.com).
